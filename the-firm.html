Our standards

How we handle your work

The same rules apply to every engagement, whether we keep your books, plan your numbers or prepare you for a raise.

  • 01

    Reviewed by a Chartered Accountant

    Every deliverable is reviewed by a Chartered Accountant before it reaches you.

  • 02

    No banking passwords

    Role-based logins to your software and read-only bank feeds; we don't ask for banking passwords.

  • 03

    ICAI Code of Ethics

    Client information is handled under the ICAI Code of Ethics and an NDA where required.

  • 04

    Written scope, fixed calendar

    Each engagement runs on a written scope and a fixed monthly calendar, set out in the engagement letter.

  • 05

    Inside your systems

    We work inside your own accounting system, so your books and records stay with you.

  • 06

    A package for your CPA

    For clients abroad, we hand a year-end package to your CPA or tax preparer, who handles the filing.
