What a month looks like

A typical monthly calendar

Exact dates are fixed in your engagement letter. This is the usual rhythm for a combined bookkeeping, FP&A and virtual CFO engagement.

1234
  1. Days 1–5

    Reconcile

    Bank, card, gateway and payroll reconciliations; open items flagged to you.

  2. Days 6–10

    Close

    Accruals and adjustments; statements reviewed by a Chartered Accountant.

  3. Days 10–15

    Report

    KPIs, budget vs actual, cash position and an updated forecast.

  4. Mid-month

    Review

    A call on the numbers, decisions ahead and lender or investor needs.

Ways to work with us

Three engagement models

Monthly engagement

Ongoing bookkeeping, FP&A and virtual CFO work on a fixed monthly calendar, with a named team and a regular review call.

Project engagement

Defined-scope work with a start and an end: fundraising readiness, feasibility studies, cap table rebuilds and books clean-up.

Dedicated team

Accountants or FP&A analysts working as an extension of your in-house finance team, in your systems and on your calendar.
